## Preservation changes and verification

Original regular-file contents are preserved byte-for-byte. Added archival material consists only of this `ARCHIVE.md` and `ARCHIVE-MANIFEST.json`, which lists the original files with their sizes, archive modes and SHA-256 hashes. Original source, bundled assets and documentation were not rewritten to point at this copy. Historical download links may therefore be broken.

GitHub Actions are disabled at repository level. Preserved build scripts/workflows and bundled executables were **not run**. Buildability, compatibility and safety have not been established by this preservation. This is **not** an automatically generated binary release.

The snapshot includes Flite, speech voices and other third-party code/data. Their existing notices, including `3RDPARTY`, are retained. In particular, this archive does not relicense all bundled components under the project-level license. No separate PolyHook or hacklib repository was published as part of this preservation task.
